# FleetIQ browser language

Browser-local vocabulary extends the [shared product glossary](https://github.com/stanimirivanov/fleetiq-platform/blob/main/GLOSSARY.md); shared product terms retain platform-owned meanings.

## Language

**Loaded page**: The bounded collection currently available in the browser; local filtering does not establish whole-tenant totals.

**Metadata review scope**: The effective-time and knowledge-cutoff selection used to inspect semantic configuration.

**Coordinate mapping**: An explicit assignment of exact properties to latitude and longitude roles with a selected encoding.

**Browser-visible evidence**: Evidence successfully obtained and presented within the current authorized browser scope.

**Sample mode**: An explicitly selected synthetic presentation, distinct from observed connected product evidence.

**Ambiguous command outcome**: A command attempt whose authoritative outcome is unknown to the browser; it is not a confirmed failure or success.

**Presentation role**: The use of evidence in the interface, such as a coordinate axis, without redefining its underlying semantic attribution.
